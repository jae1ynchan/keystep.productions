About Us

A studio built for the AI era.

Key Step Productions was founded in 2026 in Hong Kong to do one thing very well: short, sharp videos that we make with AI and publish ourselves.

Our story

We started with simple intros. A friend's channel needed an opening, a school project needed a title sequence, and there was no budget for a film crew — so we learned to make them with AI instead.

What surprised us was how much those twenty seconds changed everything else. People watched longer. The work looked deliberate. A small idea suddenly had a front door.

So we kept going. Today the studio writes, generates, cuts and publishes its own AI videos — short films, shorts and title sequences — and still does all of it in one small room.

Make it look like it belongs on a big screen — on a budget of almost nothing.

What we hold to

Four things we do not compromise on, whatever the deadline says.

Craft

Obvious AI is a wasted idea. We re-generate, re-cut and re-mix until it reads as intentional.

Momentum

Days, not months. Most videos go from script to published inside a week, and the next one starts before the last one is out.

Honesty

If a video is not good enough we say so and re-cut it — or drop it. Nothing gets published just to hit a date.

Curiosity

We build games and websites for fun. It keeps the video work from ever looking like a template.

From the founder

Key Step Productions began as a student project in Hong Kong in 2026 and has been run like a real studio ever since — schedules, deadlines and published work, not drafts.

The ambition behind it is simple and slightly unreasonable: build something that stands on its own, hold the work to a world-class standard, and keep learning in the best rooms available — in Hong Kong now, and at the best schools in the world later.
