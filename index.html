How we make a video

One pipeline, four steps, from first line to publish button.

Every video we release goes through the same four stages. It keeps the channel consistent, and it keeps us honest about what is finished.

01

Idea & script

Every video starts as one sentence and a script. Nothing gets generated until the words work on their own.

02

Storyboard & look

Shots, references, palette and pacing — planned on paper before the first render is paid for.

03

AI production

We generate the footage shot by shot and re-roll until it holds up at full size. This is the slow part, and it is worth it.

04

Edit, sound & publish

Cut, colour, music and mix by hand — then it goes out on the channel the same week it is finished.

Studio notes

Small studio, published work.

AI makes the impossible parts fast. Taste, timing, sound and honesty are still done by a person — and the work goes out where people can actually see it.

AI-first, human-finished

Generative tools give us a speed traditional production cannot match. The edit, the pacing and the sound design are still done by hand, frame by frame.

Publishing is the point

A finished video sitting in a folder does not count. Everything we make gets published, dated and left online — including the ones we would cut differently now.
